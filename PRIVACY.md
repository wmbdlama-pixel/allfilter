# AllFilter — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Published by wmbdlama**

> **In short:** AllFilter collects no data, stores no data and makes no network
> connections. It runs entirely inside Autodesk Revit on your computer.

---

## Who we are

AllFilter is an add-in for Autodesk Revit, published by wmbdlama.
You can reach us at **wmbdlama@gmail.com**.

## What data AllFilter collects

**AllFilter collects no data.** The add-in contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash
reporting, no advertising identifiers and no third-party SDK.

## What AllFilter reads

To build its filter tree, AllFilter reads the following from the model you currently have
open in Revit:

- element categories;
- parameter names and parameter values;
- the text of text notes and the text shown on tags.

This information is held in memory only, for as long as the AllFilter window is open. It is
never written to disk, never copied outside Revit and never transmitted anywhere.

## What AllFilter writes

Nothing. AllFilter does not create, modify or delete files, registry entries or model data.
Its actions are limited to selecting elements and changing temporary view visibility in
Revit, both of which you can undo with Revit's own commands.

## Network connections

AllFilter makes no network connections. It works with no internet connection at all.

## Third parties

AllFilter shares no data with anyone, because it collects none. No third party receives
data through this add-in.

## Data retention and deletion

As AllFilter stores no data, there is nothing to retain and nothing to delete. Uninstalling
the add-in removes it completely.

## Your consent

Because no personal data is collected, there is no consent to grant or revoke. If you have
questions about this policy, write to **wmbdlama@gmail.com** and we will answer within two
business days.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Autodesk App Store and its payment provider, not by AllFilter and
not by wmbdlama directly. Payment data never passes through this add-in. The handling of
that data is covered by the privacy policies of Autodesk and of the payment provider.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text is published at this address and the date above is
updated. Material changes are also noted in the release notes of the version that
introduces them.
